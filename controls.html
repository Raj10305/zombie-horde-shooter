Controls

Input Action
WASD / Arrow Keys Move
Mouse Move Aim weapon or target airstrike corridor
Left Click (Hold or Click) Fire gun or call selected air support
1 – 9, 0 or Mouse Wheel Switch between all 10 primary weapons
Q Deploy Auto-Turret
E Call Orbital Ion Laser (follows cursor)
Space / Shift Dash (brief invulnerability)
B / Tab Open Armory & Upgrades
H (Sandbox mode) Spawn Extra Mega-Horde
M Mute / Unmute Sound Effects
P / Esc Pause / Resume

Weapons & Support

Key Category Weapon Description
1 Guns M4 Assault Rifle Fast, accurate carbine bullets.
2 Guns SPAS-12 Shotgun 8-pellet wide spread with heavy knockback.
3 Guns M134 Vulcan Minigun Ultra-rapid rotary gunfire.
4 Guns Railgun Sniper Instant hyper-velocity piercing beam.
5 Heavy & Sci-Fi M2 Flamethrower Continuous napalm stream that ignites zombies.
6 Heavy & Sci-Fi RPG-7 Rocket Launcher Explosive rockets with large blast radius.
7 Heavy & Sci-Fi Tesla Arc Cannon Chain lightning jumping across multiple zombies.
8 Airplanes A-10 Warthog Jet Attack airplane strafing your aimed corridor with explosive 30mm rounds.
9 Airplanes B-52 Carpet Bomber Heavy bomber dropping a rolling line of 12+ bombs.
0 Airplanes Apache Gunship Orbiting attack helicopter with autocannon and homing rockets.
Q Tactical Support Deploy Auto-Turret Automated sentry turret locking onto nearby zombies.
E Tactical Support Orbital Ion Laser Continuous orbital laser beam following your cursor.

Combat Arenas

Arena Environment Tactical Notes
Military Outpost Concrete base & command helipad Wide open crossroad avenues, four corner bunkers, and central helipad. Good for airstrikes and long-range weapons.
Quarantine City Ruins Downtown urban ruins & cracked asphalt Destroyed skyscraper blocks, narrow kill-zone alleys, car wrecks, abandoned bus roadblocks, and toxic biohazard drums. High enemy ambush threat!
