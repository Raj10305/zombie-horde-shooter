# DEADLINE: SKYFIRE — 2D Zombie Horde Shooter

A fast-paced, zero-dependency 2D top-down zombie horde shooter where you can freely pick and combine firearms, heavy sci-fi weapons, attack airplanes, carpet bombers, gunship helicopters, automated turrets, and orbital ion lasers.

## How to Play
Double-click **`index.html`** in this folder to launch the game directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) — no server or installation required.

## Game Modes
- **Survival Waves**: All 10 primary weapons are unlocked from Wave 1. Earn cash from zombie kills and wave bonuses to upgrade guns and unlock multi-plane formations (2-jet & 3-jet A-10 squadrons, dual B-52 bombers). Defeat Necro Titan bosses every 5 waves.
- **Sandbox Chaos**: Start with `$99,999`, ultra-fast airplane & bomber cooldowns, and a **`+ HORDE [H]`** button to spawn massive swarms whenever you want.

## Controls
- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move
- **Mouse**: Aim & Left-Click (or hold) to shoot / call airstrikes at cursor
- **1 – 9, 0** or **Mouse Wheel**: Switch between all 10 primary weapons
- **Q**: Deploy Automated Twin-Barrel Sentry Turret
- **E**: Call Orbital Ion Laser (follows your cursor)
- **Space / Shift**: Tactical Dash (with brief invulnerability)
- **B / Tab**: Open Armory & Upgrade Shop
- **H** *(Sandbox)*: Spawn Extra Mega-Horde
- **M**: Mute / Unmute synthesized SFX
- **P / Esc**: Pause

## Full Arsenal
### Guns
1. **M4 Assault Rifle `[1]`** — Accurate rapid-fire carbine.
2. **SPAS-12 Shotgun `[2]`** — 8-pellet wide spread with heavy knockback.
3. **M134 Vulcan Minigun `[3]`** — Extreme fire rate rotary cannon.
4. **Railgun Sniper `[4]`** — Instant piercing hyper-velocity slug.

### Heavy & Sci-Fi
5. **M2 Flamethrower `[5]`** — Ignites zombie packs with burning napalm.
6. **RPG-7 Rocket Launcher `[6]`** — High-explosive blast rockets.
7. **Tesla Arc Cannon `[7]`** — Chain lightning jumping across multiple zombies.

### Airplanes & Air Support
8. **A-10 Warthog Jet `[8]`** — Calls a low-altitude jet firing explosive 30mm cannon strafes along your aimed corridor (Lv 3 calls 2 jets, Lv 5 calls a 3-jet formation).
9. **B-52 Carpet Bomber `[9]`** — Heavy bomber dropping a rolling line of 12+ high-explosive bombs (Lv 4+ calls dual bombers).
10. **Apache Gunship `[0]`** — Orbiting attack helicopter with rapid autocannon and twin homing Hydra rockets.

### Tactical Support
- **Auto-Turret `[Q]`** — Automated sentry turret that locks onto nearby hordes.
- **Orbital Ion Laser `[E]`** — Continuous satellite beam that sweeps across your cursor.
